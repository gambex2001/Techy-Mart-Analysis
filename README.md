# Techy-Mart-Analysis

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze Techy Mart’s sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing products and cities, and customer payment preferences, providing insights that can support better business decision-making.

## Dataset Used
[Download Excel File](https://github.com/gambex2001/Techy-Mart-Analysis/raw/refs/heads/main/Sales-Dataset.xlsx)

## Questions
Which sub-categories generate the highest sales?
Which cities contribute the most to overall sales?
How do sales change from month to month?
Which payment methods are most commonly used?
Which product categories should receive more attention based on sales performance?

## Key Insights
Markers generated the highest sales among the top 5 sub-categories shown.
Printers and Tables also recorded strong sales, while Pens had the lowest sales among the displayed sub-categories.
Orlando recorded the highest sales among the top 5 cities, at approximately $5.03M.
Buffalo followed with about $4.84M, while San Francisco recorded approximately $4.82M.
Miami and Rochester both recorded approximately $4.47M.
The monthly sales trend fluctuated throughout the year, with sales generally staying around the $0.10M–$0.16M range.
The payment-mode chart shows that Debit Card and Credit Card account for substantial portions of sales, while COD, EMI and UPI make up the remaining share.

## Procedure
Imported and cleaned the sales dataset.
Created PivotTables to summarize sales.
Analyzed sales by sub-category, city, month and payment mode.
Created charts to visualize the results.
Added slicers for interactive filtering.
Combined the visuals into an interactive dashboard.

## Visual Design
Bar chart → Top 5 sales by sub-category
Column chart → Top 5 sales by city
Line chart → Monthly sales trend
Doughnut chart → Payment mode distribution
Slicer → Category filtering

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides a clear overview of Techy Mart's sales performance and helps identify strong-performing products, locations, sales periods and payment channels.
